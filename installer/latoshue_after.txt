Latos Hue (修图助手) is installed. / 已安装完成。

What happens next / 接下来：
1. If Lightroom Classic is open, restart it (or open File > Plug-in Manager, select LrMCP and click Reload).
   如果 Lightroom Classic 正开着，请重启它（或在 文件 > 增效工具管理器 里选中 LrMCP 后点“重新载入增效工具”）。

2. Latos Hue uses the Claude (or Codex) command line that is already installed and signed in on this computer.
   It does not log in for you and does not need to be registered anywhere.
   Latos Hue 用的是你电脑上已经装好并登录的 Claude（或 Codex）命令行，不会替你登录，也不用另外登记。

3. The first time you open Latos Hue, the "Environment check" tells you what is still missing
   (photo folder, Lightroom, plug-in, AI assistant) and what to do about it.
   第一次打开 Latos Hue 时，“环境检查”会告诉你还缺什么（照片文件夹、Lightroom、插件、AI 助手）以及怎么办。

Your photos stay on this computer. While retouching, the preview of the selected photo is sent to the AI service you chose.
你的照片只在本机处理；修图时，选中照片的预览图会发送给你所选的 AI 服务。

Latos Hue is open source and is not an Adobe product. 开源软件，非 Adobe 官方产品。
