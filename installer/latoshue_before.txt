Latos Hue (修图助手) - please read / 请先看一眼
================================================

1. This software is open source (AGPL-3.0, the next page shows the full text).
   本软件是开源软件，许可证是 AGPL-3.0（下一页是全文）。

2. Latos Hue does NOT contain any Adobe software and is NOT an Adobe product. You need your own Lightroom Classic.
   Latos Hue 不含任何 Adobe 软件，也不是 Adobe 官方产品；Lightroom Classic 需要你自己已经装好。

3. Your photos are processed on this computer only. When you ask the AI to retouch a photo, a preview of the selected
   photo is sent to the AI service you chose (Claude or Codex). Nothing else is uploaded.
   你的照片只在本机处理。修图时，选中照片的预览图会发送给你所选的 AI 服务（Claude 或 Codex），除此之外不会上传任何东西。

4. The installer puts a small plug-in into Lightroom's plug-in folder so the two programs can talk to each other
   (%APPDATA%\Adobe\Lightroom\Modules\LrMcp.lrplugin). It does not change your photos or your catalog.
   安装程序会把一个小插件放进 Lightroom 的插件文件夹，让两个程序能互相通信；不会改动你的照片和目录。

5. No administrator rights are needed. Nothing starts with Windows, and no system setting is changed.
   不需要管理员权限；不会开机自启，也不会改动系统设置。
